# NovaGate G200 Installation Guide
## Before you begin
Use a 12 to 24 V DC power supply rated for at least 10 W. Mount the gateway on a DIN rail or a wall. The operating temperature range is -20 to 60 degrees Celsius.
## Connect and power on
1. Connect an Ethernet cable from the LAN port to your network.
2. Connect the power supply. The power LED turns green within 30 seconds.
3. Open a browser and go to 192.168.1.1.
4. Sign in and set a new administrator password when prompted.
## LED states
Solid green means normal operation. Blinking red means no network link. Solid blue means the gateway is in recovery mode.
