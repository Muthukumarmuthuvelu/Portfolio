NovaGate G200 Troubleshooting

The web interface does not open

Check that your computer is on the same network as the gateway and browse to 192.168.1.1. If the page still does not load, power cycle the gateway and wait for the green LED.

The LED is blinking red

A blinking red LED means no network link (error E101). Check the Ethernet cable and the switch port, then restart the gateway.

The gateway cannot reach the cloud

Confirm that the date and time are correct and that outbound port 8883 is open on your firewall. Wrong time settings cause error E204.
