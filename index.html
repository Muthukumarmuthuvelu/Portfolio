What I bring to a leadership role

Four connected strengths that show up in every team I have run.

Product ownership

Requirements, user stories and acceptance criteria that turn stakeholder needs into roadmap delivery. Backlog prioritisation, sprint planning and release readiness for hardware and WebUI/cloud products, with firmware, software and cloud dependencies coordinated in JIRA.

Knowledge management

Information architecture, taxonomy, metadata, review workflows and lifecycle management. Structured authoring and reusable content models make enterprise knowledge governed, consistent and easy to find.

Partner enablement
